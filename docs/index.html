How to read tmrank

GOAT ranking

Who built the strongest all-time case from a mix of peak, longevity, sustained level, and major results.

Current ranking

Who looks strongest right now, with inactivity gradually making old strength count less.

Title points

Credit for major placements only. Useful on its own, but not the whole ranking by itself.

Profiles

Alternate lenses for formats like campaigns, Kacky, ZrT cups, and World Cups, each using only events from that category.

Modeling choices

The default ranking is mainly an esports view. Event inclusion, event weighting, and majors definition are deliberate modeling choices, especially across very different Trackmania formats.

Hall of fame

GOAT Top 20

Strongest all-time players, determined by blending: Prime = best sustained high point. Elite area = how much top-level strength a player built over time. Median = typical active-month level. Title points = major placements only.

Right now

Current Top 10

Strongest active players right now. Good recent results against strong fields help most, while long inactivity lowers confidence.

Major résumé

Major Titles Leaders

Best major-event resumés on their own. This is separate from the main rating model and only tracks the curated majors list.

Era leadership

Rating Leader Timeline

Who held the #1 rating over time, month by month, grouped into continuous eras and shown as a compact ribbon.

Other lenses

Alternate Profiles

Side views for formats that reward different things. They are meant to show different ranking philosophies, not pretend one list fits every corner of Trackmania.
